# Guidance Without Enforcement

Keep profile-applicable SHOULD and MAY guidance separate from compliance
findings and summary counts. Cite its source and condition, and label it
**Recommended** or **Optional**. Do not assign a rule status or change the
overall result.

Useful recommendations from the pinned
[definition of done](https://github.com/Azure-Samples/azd-template-artifacts/blob/22de60335bd77269a2e952cd0e3e8fab56ed4aa1/docs/development-guidelines/definition-of-done.md#should)
include testing the end-user setup path, covering changed logic, explaining
authentication and secrets handling, and preferring Entra ID or managed
identities where supported. Do not claim those practices were verified by this
audit. Dev containers, CI/CD, screenshots, and demo videos remain optional
unless an applicable rule requires them. Gallery preview images are
**Recommended**, not required by AZD-AWESOME-005.
